## 1. Scientific Mission & Theoretical Anchor
Manuscript v87 posits a 9.2% over-rejection frequency along the unclustered homoscedastic pathway, attributing this anomaly to the failure of the $\chi^2$ approximation under $t_7$ innovations (Appendix Figure A1, Section "Empirical Boundaries"). Stream R02b resizes the sampling geometry to 1000 independent trajectories per grid point while sweeping the degrees of freedom $\nu$. This architecture systematically investigates whether the reported divergence operates as a genuine tail phenomenon. The executive verdict delivers a double falsification (Class A, D3). First, the measured rejection frequency at $\nu=7$ equals 5.8%, and its surrounding Wilson confidence interval solidly encapsulates the 5% nominal threshold. Second, the theoretical mechanism asserted by the manuscript—that $t_7$ innovations deprive $\varepsilon_t^2$ of its fourth moment—is mathematically incorrect, because the finite variance condition $E[\varepsilon_t^4] < \infty$ remains strictly satisfied for all $\nu > 4$.

## 2. Empirical Methodology & Statistical Diagnostics
The experimental protocol executes the lag-20 Ljung-Box diagnostic across sequences of 8000 observations, generating 1,000 independent arrays per coordinate spanning $\nu \in \{5, 6, 7, 8.5, 12, 30\}$. The squared operational stream rejects the null hypothesis at 8.8% ($\nu=5$) and 7.9% ($\nu=6$), both strictly excluding the 0.05 threshold. At the targeted $\nu=7$ configuration, the rate drops to 5.8% (95% Wilson `[4.5%, 7.4%]`). The paired negative control evaluating raw innovations maintains the 5% nominal boundary strictly across the entire spectrum (yielding 5.7% at $\nu=5$, 4.3% at $\nu=6$, and 5.7% at $\nu=7$). This negative control firmly establishes that the testing implementation remains mathematically sound. It isolates the distributional distortion exclusively within the squaring transformation.

## 4. Analytical Boundaries & Methodological Scope
The 9.2% historical figure did not represent fabricated data; it constituted a routine stochastic draw originating from a severely underpowered sample space. Given an underlying 5.8% probability, recording 11 or more threshold breaches out of 120 attempts carries an 8.9% mathematical likelihood. The original analytical defect resides entirely within extrapolating systematic dynamics from inadequate sampling volume. The genuine over-rejection phenomenon exists and yields statistically significant measurements, yet its boundary resides strictly at $\nu \le 6$. While this module mathematically establishes that the manuscript's causal attribution is flawed, the actual root mechanism driving the heavy-tail divergence remains unidentified. Isolating whether this anomaly stems from finite-sample Berry-Esseen convergence delays or from asymptotic quantile deterioration requires a dedicated infinite-horizon sweep, a task explicitly allocated to stream R02c.
